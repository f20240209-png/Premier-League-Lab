Behind the estimate · methodology and data coverage

What the model knows

Ordinary least squares regression with standardised numeric features and an encoded position. Training seasons: {{ r.train_seasons|join(', ') }} (start years). Training valuations end {{ r.train_through }}. No player names, IDs, current valuations or later-season statistics enter the model.

{{ r.manifest.eligibility }} The same player can appear in earlier training seasons and the later test season ({{ r.returning_players }} test players did); this evaluates a later season, not exclusively unseen players.

{{ r.manifest.position_note }} Contract length, injuries, reputation and non-EPL performances are not modelled. Negative predictions are floored at zero; {{ r.negative_predictions_clipped }} test predictions required this.
